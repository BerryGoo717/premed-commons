Quick questions

Who is Premed Commons for?

Any student working toward medical school, at any stage. Whether you are choosing a major or preparing your application, there is a place for you.

Does it cost anything?

Add your pricing details here. If membership is free, say so plainly.

Do I need to be a biology or chemistry major?

No. Medical schools accept applicants from every major as long as the required prerequisite courses are completed. Check the requirements of the schools you are interested in.
