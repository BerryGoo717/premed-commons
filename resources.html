Activity balance

Enter your hours to see a picture of your experiences. There is no score and no pass or fail. It is just a way to notice where you have built a lot and where you might want to spend time next.

  • Your hours
  • Common starting range

Your hours are saved only in this browser on this device. We never see them. The ranges come from rough guidance shared by premed advising offices (for example Northwestern and UConn). They are not requirements, there is no standard number for research, and every school looks at the whole application. In an AAMC survey, schools said they value what you gain from an experience more than the number of hours.
