Why medicine?

A reflection builder with guided prompts to help you find your own story. Answer the ones that speak to you, skip the rest, and come back any time. Nothing here is required, and nothing is graded.

Scroll inside the prompt panel to move through the reflections. Your answers save automatically.

Your answers are saved only in this browser on this device, and we never see them. If you clear your browser data they will be gone, so download a copy now and then. These prompts are meant to give you raw material, not a draft. Your personal statement should be written in your own words and your own voice.
